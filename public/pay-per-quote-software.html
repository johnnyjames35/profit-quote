Pay-per-quote software for occasional jobs

Some weeks you need one proper quote, not another monthly commitment. ProfitQuote gives UK tradespeople a £5 pay-as-you-go option after a seven-day unlimited free trial.

What does £5 buy?

One credit to create and save a new quote using the existing ProfitQuote builder. Describe your job, enter your rates, review materials and labour, check forgotten costs and contingency, then approve the margin and customer document. Your saved quote stays available to view, download or email; those actions do not use another credit. Deleting a quote does not restore a spent credit.

When does a monthly plan make sense?

For one to three quotes in a month, pay as you go costs £5–£15. Four pay-as-you-go quotes cost £20, so Starter at £19/month can be better value if you need four to six. If you regularly need more than six, Pro is £29/month for unlimited quotes. Starter resets on your billing anniversary, with no rollover. Subscriptions renew until cancelled.
