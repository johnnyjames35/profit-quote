Questions before you start

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit expressed as a percentage of cost. Margin is profit expressed as a percentage of selling price. For example, £800 cost and £1,000 selling price gives £200 profit: 25% markup but 20% margin, before any costs you have left out.
