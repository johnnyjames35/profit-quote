Measures that mean what they say

  • GA4 users / sessions: hostname-filtered recorded traffic, including www. Admin and owner-dashboard paths excluded. Owner visits elsewhere, consent and blockers affect coverage. Users are not deduplicated across apps.
  • Search clicks / impressions: finalized web-search totals for the verified website property. Uses Pacific dates. No impression means no meaningful average position.
  • New registrations: still-present app accounts created in the chosen period, or Kelvori workspaces. Deleted historical records are not included. Not a count of verified customers or sales.
  • Registered now: currently existing records regardless of the selected date range. Identified test addresses and LatePay admin records are excluded. Other tests may remain.
  • Useful activity: ProfitQuote saved account quotes; CallbackApp missed-call texts recorded as sent (not delivery confirmation); LatePay invoices added (not our revenue); Kelvori stored workspace assessments updated (not all anonymous assessments or a historical completion log).
  • Comparison: GA4 and search show the immediately preceding period of the same length. Current and previous figures must both be available. No growth percentage is invented when the baseline is zero.
  • Freshness: refresh retrieves current sources, with a 60-second server cache to avoid duplicate Google calls. No data is collected by leaving this page open. Choose Today for current app activity; Google reporting can lag.
  • Privacy: all reporting routes require authorization. Only aggregate counts reach this page. No Stripe connection, revenue reporting or customer identities are included.
