Before you send a price

Catch the work that is easy to leave out.

Sequence the labour

Allow for strip-out, preparation, installation and finishing. Include subcontractor costs, return visits and the time you spend coordinating the work.

Check the site costs

Review skips, waste runs, access equipment, protection and delivery charges. Small consumables and collection time can be easy to miss beside larger material orders.

Describe the unknowns

Record assumptions about existing services, surfaces and hidden defects. Add appropriate contingency and explain exclusions; confirm extra work with the customer before proceeding.

Questions before you start

Can it price an extension or refurbishment?

You can structure the labour, materials and other costs for a building job. You still need a clear scope, reliable measurements, supplier prices and subcontractor figures; review each item before agreeing the price.

What happens if the customer changes the scope?

ProfitQuote supports job variations. Review the extra labour, materials and impact on the original work, then agree the changed scope and price with the customer. Do not absorb additional work into your original margin by accident.
