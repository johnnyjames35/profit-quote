Before you send a price

Catch the work that is easy to leave out.

Define the points and scope

Record rooms, circuits, accessories and customer requirements. Separate standard fittings from customer-selected upgrades and confirm who supplies them.

Price the complete working time

Allow for preparation, first fix, second fix, testing, documentation and return visits. Occupied rooms and restricted access can change the duration.

Include the less visible costs

Review cables, fixings, consumables, access equipment and waste. Clarify chasing and making good so your quote does not silently include somebody else’s finishing work.

Questions before you start

Does ProfitQuote produce electrical certificates?

The professional PDF is a customer quote. Testing and certification work should be allowed for in your scope and costs, but ProfitQuote does not replace your testing equipment, certification system or professional assessment.

Can I quote a smaller electrical job?

Yes. Describe the actual work and include travel, setup, testing and any return visit as appropriate. A small materials bill does not necessarily mean a short job, so review the total labour allowance before sending the quote.
