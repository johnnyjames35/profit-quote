Before you send a price

Catch the work that is easy to leave out.

Time beyond the tools

Include measuring up, collection runs, travel, preparation and clearing away. If two people are needed, account for both people’s time.

Materials beyond the main item

Add fixings, consumables, delivery and waste allowances alongside the main materials. Check supplier prices before you approve the figure.

Money left after the job

Review overheads, contingency and your target margin separately. A busy diary is not enough if each job leaves too little to cover the business.

Questions before you start

Can I use it for more than one trade?

Yes. You can price labour-and-material work across trades. Choose the relevant job type and review the scope for each job; the cost prompts are a starting point, not a substitute for your site knowledge.

Will it tell me the right price to charge?

You set and approve the figures. ProfitQuote helps organise the job pricing and flag a tight margin, but your rates, supplier costs and assessment of the work determine the quote.
