Free job pricing checklist and margin calculator

Before you send a price, check that it pays for the whole job. Work through the checklist, then test your selling price. No email or signup required.

Check your margin

Use costs excluding recoverable VAT. Include non-recoverable tax within your costs. The result is a selling price before VAT and profit before any costs you have left out.

Formula: selling price = cost ÷ (1 − margin ÷ 100). A 20% markup on £1,000 gives a £1,200 price and 16.7% margin; a 20% margin needs a £1,250 price.

Your before-you-quote checklist

Put the checklist to work

For a bathroom installation, customer-supplied fittings do not remove your labour, small parts or disposal costs. For a rewire, testing and making good may add time beyond the cable installation. For a refurbishment, include the visits between trades. Use your inspection and experience to decide what belongs in your quote.
