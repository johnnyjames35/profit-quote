How to Write a Professional Builder's Quote (Free Template & Examples)

By John James · Founder of ProfitQuote · 7 min read · Last updated: 27 July 2026

A scruffy, hand-written quote on the back of a diary page and a clean, professional quotation can be for the exact same job, at the exact same price — and still win completely different results. Customers can't judge your workmanship before you start, so the quote is often the only thing they have to judge you on. Here's how to write one that wins the job and protects your profit.

What Every Professional Quote Needs

Before writing your quotation, make sure you've included every hidden business expense — read 25 Costs Tradespeople Forget to Include so nothing slips through unbilled. Whatever trade you're in, a proper quotation should always include:

  • Your business name, logo, address and contact details
  • A unique quote number and the date issued
  • The customer's name and the job address (if different)
  • A clear description of the work to be carried out
  • A breakdown of costs — materials, labour, and any separate items
  • What's excluded (so there's no argument later about what wasn't included)
  • Payment terms, including deposit amount if one is required
  • How long the price is valid for (e.g. 30 days)
  • Your signature line and space for the customer to accept

Mistakes That Make a Quote Look Unprofessional

1. No breakdown, just a single number

A single lump sum with no detail makes it hard for a customer to compare you fairly against other quotes — and hard for you to justify the price if they query it.

2. No exclusions listed

Without a clear "what's not included," any unexpected extra becomes an argument rather than a straightforward variation.

3. No expiry date

Material prices move. A quote with no expiry can come back to bite you months later at a price you can no longer honour.

4. Sent as a text message or verbally only

A written, numbered quotation is what protects you if there's ever a dispute — and it simply looks more credible than a price given over the phone.
