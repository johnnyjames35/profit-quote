About ProfitQuote

Practical quoting help for UK tradespeople.

ProfitQuote helps tradespeople turn job details into professional, profit-checked customer quotes while keeping control of every figure.

Who operates ProfitQuote?

ProfitQuote is operated by Big Bulldog UK Ltd (company number 15946705). John James is the founder of ProfitQuote and the named author of its practical Trade Guides.

Why it exists

The product was created to make structured quoting easier: capturing labour, materials, overheads, contingency and profit before a quote reaches the customer. ProfitQuote does not set a price automatically or replace a tradesperson's judgement.

How the guides are written

The Trade Guides explain the same practical pricing and quoting principles used in the product. They are educational material rather than accounting, tax or legal advice. Pages identify their author and update date so readers can judge their context.
